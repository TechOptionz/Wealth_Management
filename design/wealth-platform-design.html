Rules the UI follows

Every total carries a date. Net worth, valuations and loan balances show their as-of date beside the number; anything older than 12 months is chipped as stale.

Every total can be explained. KPI tiles link to a drill-down that lists the records behind the figure, down to the source document.

Status is never colour alone. Each chip has an icon and words, so it survives colour-blindness and greyscale printing (NFR-07).

Unavailable beats zero. A missing denominator shows “Unavailable”, never 0%.

Suggestions look different from facts. Confidence bars and “Confirm” buttons mark anything a model or matcher proposed; posted records have neither.

Scope is always visible. The sidebar scope pill shows which entities, properties and period the screen covers; restricted users see a “limited view” variant.

One hero, everything else quiet. Only the primary KPI uses the charcoal fill; other tiles are white with a single accent so the eye lands in one place.
